Research opportunities

ATL welcomes students interested in computational and experimental materials research. Current projects involve alloys for nuclear and extreme environments, chemical disorder, interfaces, defects, amorphization, lattice dynamics, and materials informatics.

Projects may involve DFT, molecular dynamics, machine-learning interatomic potentials, scientific computing, alloy synthesis, or materials characterization.

Research training

Independent research

Students develop increasing ownership of research questions and methods. Training emphasizes physical reasoning, reproducible calculations and experiments, scientific writing, conference presentations, and peer-reviewed publications.
