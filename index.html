About

Alloy theory, computation, and experiments

I am an Assistant Professor in the Department of Mechanical Engineering at the University of Wyoming. My group studies refractory alloys, radiation effects, oxidation, chemical disorder, amorphization, interfaces, lattice dynamics, and transport.

We combine first-principles calculations, molecular dynamics, machine-learning interatomic potentials, CALPHAD, alloy synthesis, and materials characterization to connect atomic-scale mechanisms with measurable materials response.

Research at a glance

Research areas

01

Extreme-Environment Materials

Refractory alloys, oxidation-resistant materials, radiation effects, and structural materials for nuclear and high-temperature environments.

02

Chemical Disorder & Interfaces

Short-range order, segregation, amorphization, grain boundaries, complexions, and interstitial–solute interactions.

03

Lattice Dynamics & Transport

Phonons, vibrational stability, diffusion, thermal transport, and composition-dependent lattice dynamics.

04

Computational Materials Design

DFT, molecular dynamics, machine-learning interatomic potentials, CALPHAD, and computational–experimental alloy design.
